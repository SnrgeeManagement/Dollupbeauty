Doll-Up Hair Salon Company (“Doll-Up,” “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, maintain, protect, and disclose information when you visit or interact with our website (“Website”), submit a salon suite application, request information or a tour, contact us, interact with StyleSync, or otherwise communicate with us electronically.

By accessing or using our Website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the Website.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect:

  • Through the Website;
  • Through contact forms and inquiries;
  • Through salon suite applications and availability requests;
  • Through requests to schedule a tour;
  • Through StyleSync-related inquiries;
  • Through email, text message, or other electronic communications associated with the Website;
  • Through cookies and similar technologies; and
  • Through other online interactions where this Privacy Policy is displayed or referenced.

This Privacy Policy does not necessarily apply to information collected by independently operated beauty professionals featured through StyleSync or operating within Doll-Up Beauty Suites. Those professionals may maintain their own business and privacy practices.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • First and last name;
  • Email address;
  • Telephone number;
  • Business or professional name;
  • Social media handle or website;
  • Professional or beauty-service specialty;
  • Licensing or certification information;
  • Years of professional experience;
  • Information regarding your current beauty business;
  • Salon suite preferences;
  • Desired move-in date;
  • Information submitted through a salon suite application;
  • Tour requests;
  • Messages, questions, and other information submitted through contact forms; and
  • Records of correspondence between you and Doll-Up Hair Salon Company.

We request only information reasonably related to the purpose for which it is collected.

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, banking information, medical information, or other highly sensitive personal information through general Website forms unless we specifically request such information through an appropriate secure process.

3. Information Collected Automatically

When you access or use our Website, certain information may be collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies.

This information may include:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type;
  • Device type;
  • Operating system;
  • Approximate geographic location derived from your IP address;
  • Pages viewed;
  • Date and time of Website visits;
  • Referring or exit pages;
  • Links or Website features used; and
  • General Website usage and performance information.

We may use this information to operate, secure, maintain, and improve our Website and understand how visitors interact with our content.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Website may use cookies, pixels, web beacons, analytics technologies, and similar tools.

Cookies are small files stored on your device that may assist with Website functionality, analytics, preferences, security, and performance.

You may configure your browser to refuse or limit cookies. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or availability of portions of the Website. Third-party service providers integrated into our Website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

5. How We Use Your Information

Doll-Up Hair Salon Company may use information collected through the Website to:

  • Operate and maintain the Website;
  • Respond to questions, messages, and inquiries;
  • Review salon suite applications;
  • Communicate regarding suite availability;
  • Schedule or coordinate salon suite tours;
  • Evaluate prospective salon suite occupants;
  • Provide information regarding Doll-Up Beauty Suites;
  • Facilitate StyleSync-related communications;
  • Provide requested information or services;
  • Communicate regarding applications, appointments, tours, or other business matters;
  • Maintain appropriate business records;
  • Improve our Website, services, and customer experience;
  • Analyze Website usage and performance;
  • Protect against fraudulent, unauthorized, or unlawful activity;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations;
  • Enforce our agreements and Website Terms of Use; and
  • Carry out another purpose disclosed to you when the information is collected or with your consent.

6. Salon Suite Applications

If you apply for a suite at Doll-Up Beauty Suites, we may collect information reasonably necessary to evaluate your application.

Application information may include your contact information, professional experience, business information, services offered, licensing or certification status, current business operations, desired move-in date, and other information you voluntarily provide.

Submission of an application does not guarantee approval or suite availability. Application information may be retained as reasonably necessary for our business records, to evaluate your application, communicate with you, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, or administer a subsequent business relationship.

7. StyleSync

Our Website may feature beauty professionals through StyleSync, our professional network or directory.

Information appearing in a public StyleSync profile may include a professional's name, photograph, business name, specialties, professional biography, social media information, booking information, or other business information authorized for publication. Beauty professionals operating independently may collect information directly from their own clients through booking platforms, websites, payment processors, or other systems.

Unless otherwise stated, Doll-Up Hair Salon Company does not control information independently collected by those professionals. Clients should review the privacy practices of the individual professional or applicable booking provider when appropriate.

We may also disclose aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify an individual.
