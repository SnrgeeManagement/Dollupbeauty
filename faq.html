What suites do you offer? +

We offer two suite types. The Stylist Suite is equipped for hairstylists with a shampoo bowl and styling station. The Beauty Suite is perfect for esthetics, lashes, brows & makeup.

SEE SUITES →︎
How much does a suite cost? +

Both the Stylist Suite and the Beauty Suite are $275 per week, or $1,100 per month.

Who can apply? +

Independent beauty professionals, including hair stylists, locticians, braiders, makeup and brow artists, estheticians, and lash technicians. Applicants must be at least 18 years old.

How do I apply? +

Fill out our suite application with your contact details, the suite you’re interested in, your specialty, and a little about your business. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or suite availability.

Are the suites private? +

Yes. Every suite is lockable and built for privacy, so you can create a curated experience for your clients.

Can I personalize my suite? +

Your suite is your blank canvas. Personalize your space, build your brand, and create an experience your clients will remember.

Can I schedule a tour? +

Yes. Send us a message and our team will set up a time for you to see the space.
